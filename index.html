Hitlers-Boy

A theatre play about history, ideology, and the question of personal responsibility.

Discover the Play
The Production

A Story That Raises Questions

What happens when a young person becomes drawn into an ideology? What roles do belonging, fear, and the desire for recognition play?

Hitlers-Boy explores a difficult chapter of German history. The play invites audiences to reflect on conformity, personal choices, and the responsibility of every individual.

Understanding history also means asking questions about the present.

Historical Context

Remember. Question. Understand.

The era of National Socialism demonstrates how propaganda, exclusion, and authoritarian power can influence individuals and entire societies.

Engaging with this past remains essential: for critical historical awareness, for humanity, and for a democratic future.
